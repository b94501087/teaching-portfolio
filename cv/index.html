教學理念Teaching Philosophy

以敘事為驅動的「設計即研究」教學法：將社會與空間的張力轉譯為建築論證，從物件尺度延伸至城市地景。A narrative-driven, research-by-design pedagogy that transforms social and spatial tensions into architectural arguments across scales, from object to territory.

設計理論與教學方法Design Theory & Pedagogy

設計理論Design Theory

  • 議題先於形式設計從社會與基地的矛盾出發——邊界、廢墟、生態、記憶與權力——而不是從造型出發。Issue before formDesign begins with social and site contradictions (borders, ruins, ecology, memory, power) rather than with shape.
  • 空間敘事學空間是可被閱讀的文本；以劇場、電影與文學為方法，透過序列、事件與身體經驗組織空間。Spatial narratologySpace is read as a text. Theatre, film and literature serve as methods, and space is composed through sequence, event and bodily experience.
  • 跨尺度思考同一套設計邏輯，從裝置（S）、單體建築（M）、建築群（L）一路檢驗到城市地景（XL）。Trans-scalar thinkingA single design logic is tested from installation (S) to building (M), cluster (L) and urban landscape (XL).
  • 跨領域類型建築、地景、城市、室內與舞台設計彼此流動，沒有硬性邊界。Disciplinary fluidityArchitecture, landscape, urbanism, interior design and scenography flow into one another, without hard borders.

教學方法Pedagogy

  • 大學部：建立設計語言從物件、材料與身體尺度入手，訓練觀察、概念轉譯，以及折疊、切割、堆疊等形式操作。Undergraduate: building a design languageWork begins at the scale of object, material and body, training observation, conceptual translation and formal operations such as folding, cutting and stacking.
  • 碩士：研究型設計建立清晰的批判立場；以圖解與圖面作為研究工具，將理論轉化為可檢驗的空間策略；作品集是一篇以圖面寫成的論文。Master's: research by designEach project takes a clear critical position. Diagrams and drawings act as research instruments, theory becomes a testable spatial strategy, and the portfolio reads as a thesis written in drawings.
  • 蘇格拉底式引導先問「為什麼」，再問「怎麼做」；作品的作者始終是學生，教師的角色是導師（Mentor）。Socratic mentoringWhy comes before how. Authorship always stays with the student; the teacher acts as a mentor.
  • 作品集即論證版面敘事、圖面層級與文字都是設計的一部分，讓評審在幾分鐘內讀懂整個論證。Portfolio as argumentLayout narrative, graphic hierarchy and text are part of the design, so that a jury can follow the argument within minutes.
