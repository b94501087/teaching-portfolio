# Board images — credits

Every image under `assets/board/` is a project board from the teaching portfolio, one folder per project,
named after its board slug — for example:

- `chimera-haven/` — *Chimera Haven — From Lefebvre to Lingnan Gardens* (student project boards)
- `sz-hk-border/` — *Revisiting Thirty Years Later — The Disappearing Shenzhen–Hong Kong Border* (student project boards)

Where each image comes from, and how it was cropped, is recorded in that project's manifest in `build/import/`.

No stock or sample images are used. The twelve sample projects and their placeholder images
(Unsplash covers, Wikimedia Commons photos and drawings), together with their credits, were removed on 2026-10-08.

Third-party material removed from the sz-hk-border boards before publishing:

- cover (`01`): only the hand-drawn plan is used; the portrait and book cover on the original board are excluded
- `03`: the “Online farm” tablet photo, the MIT CSAIL autonomous-boat photo (with its text) and the Water Rental photo inset
- `05`, `06`: the round photo insets of the axonometric drawings
- `07`, `08` (model display): cropped so that no person appears
